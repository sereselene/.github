# RS3 Plugins and API Developer Library

Hi I'm Sereselene and welcome to my github! I build and maintain plugins for RS3, focused on quality of life improvements that fit into how you already play, along with libraries for other plugin developers. Feedback and ideas are always welcome.

Guides, install steps and everything else live on **[sereselene.dev](https://sereselene.dev)**.

## Plugins

| Plugin | What it does | Where to get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Menu Entry Swapper | Reorder, hide and recolour your right click menus, and choose what your left click and shift click do | Plugin Hub |
| Farming Timer | Keeps track of your farming patches and Player Owned Farm animals, with ready times and teleports for your farm run | Coming soon to mod.io |
| Runic Attunement Helper | Works out which runecrafting trips and consumables earn you the most | Coming soon to mod.io |
| Fashionscape | Colour picking and palette ideas for your outfits | Coming soon to mod.io |
| Skills Calculator | Plans your route to a target level and tracks your XP as you train | In development |

## For plugin developers

| Library | What it does |
| --- | --- |
