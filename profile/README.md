# Sereselene

Hi, I'm Sereselene. This is where my RS3 plugins and plugin libraries live.

Install guides and docs are on **[sereselene.dev](https://sereselene.dev)**.

## Plugins

| Plugin | What it does | Get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Menu Entry Swapper | Reorder, hide and recolour your right click menus, and pick your left click and shift click | Plugin Hub |
| Farming Timer | Tracks your farming patches and Player Owned Farm animals, with teleports for your farm run | Coming soon |
| Runic Attunement Helper | Shows which runecrafting trips and consumables earn you the most | Coming soon |
| Fashionscape | Pick a colour and find every wearable item that matches it | Coming soon |
| Skills Calculator | Plans your route to a target level and tracks your XP as you train | In development |
